## Tips
- Landscape orientation works best (wider than tall).
- ~1000×600 px is plenty; the app scales them down.
- Copyright-free sources: unsplash.com, pexels.com, pixabay.com
- Search terms: "Indian farmer", "wheat field India", "agriculture data", "rural India"
